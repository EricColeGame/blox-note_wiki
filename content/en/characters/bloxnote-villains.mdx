## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes BloxNote villains different from antagonists in other deduction games?
Unlike generic social deduction titles where eliminations happen instantly via close-range melee, **BloxNote villains** utilize asynchronous elimination mechanics. They must discover both the true name and face of a victim, allowing for psychological mind games, scheduled eliminations, and remote assassinations from across the map.

### How do I unlock the Shinigami Eyes when playing as Kira?
You can unlock Shinigami Eyes by opening your notebook interface and interacting with the floating Shinigami companion. Confirming the deal immediately cuts your maximum health bar in half in exchange for revealing the real player names of everyone in your direct line of sight.

### Can there be multiple BloxNote villains in a single round?
Yes. Depending on lobby size and custom server configurations, matches can feature a primary Kira alongside a Second Kira or a Corrupted Official. These players must carefully coordinate without exposing their identities to each other or the investigative team.
