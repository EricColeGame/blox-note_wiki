## Complete BloxNote Characters Roster & Ability Breakdown

To succeed in high-stakes lobbies, players need an in-depth view of how individual **BloxNote characters** operate under pressure. Below is a detailed breakdown of the primary roles available in the current meta.

### 1. Master Detective (L-Archetype)
The Master Detective represents the pinnacle of investigative power. Equipped with advanced surveillance tools and enhanced analytical reasoning, this role excels at isolating suspicious behavior from afar.

* **Primary Ability — Fingerprint Sweep:** Scans interactive objects (doors, evidence drops, computers) to reveal recent user IDs.
* **Secondary Ability — Interrogation Lockdown:** Forces a target player into a temporary interrogation zone, disabling their skill usage for 6 seconds.
* **Passive — High Deduction:** Receives real-time UI alerts whenever a suspicious item is triggered nearby.

### 2. Note Wielder (Kira-Archetype)
The central antagonist in every round, the Note Wielder relies on line of sight, deception, and timing.

* **Primary Ability — Scribble Execution:** Begins writing a discovered name. Requires 5 seconds of uninterrupted focus to trigger an elimination.
* **Secondary Ability — False Alibi:** Generates a decoy task completion or temporarily spoofed player coordinates on the global map.
* **Passive — Supernatural Aura:** Immune to passive mental strain and movement speed penalties during the final phase of a round.

### 3. Forensic Specialist
A support-heavy investigative persona focused on turning crime scenes into actionable intel.

* **Primary Ability — DNA Synthesis:** Collects residual traces from eliminated allies to reveal the general faction of the culprit.
* **Secondary Ability — Revive Stasis:** Places a critically injured civilian into medical stasis, delaying elimination timers.
* **Passive — Scene Preservation:** Traps and marks footprints around recent incident zones.

### 4. Shadow Disciple (Misa-Archetype)
An aggressive supporter for the Kira faction who can sacrifice personal defense for immense intel-gathering perks.

* **Primary Ability — Crimson Vision:** Highlights all active player names within immediate line of sight at the cost of 25% max stamina.
* **Secondary Ability — Distraction Protocol:** Disables global comms or triggers a false emergency alarm across the map.
* **Passive — Devotion:** Grants the Note Wielder a passive movement boost when operating within 15 meters.

## Strategy Guide: How to Master Key BloxNote Characters

Picking the right role is only half the battle. Executing tactical movement, resource management, and social deduction will secure consistent wins.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------+
|                  Core Survival Loop                     |
|                                                         |
|  [Gather Intel] -> [Verify Alibis] -> [Execute Action]  |
|         ^                                     |         |
|         +-------------------------------------+         |
+---------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Pro Tips for Playing the Note Wielder
1. **Never Channel in Open Hallways:** Writing a target's name requires several seconds of standing stationary. Always position behind props or inside maintenance rooms.
2. **Coordinate with the Disciple:** Let your Shadow Disciple scout names with Crimson Vision while you stay undetected in crowd clusters.
3. **Pace Your Eliminations:** Eliminating targets back-to-back creates predictable patterns that make it easy for the Master Detective to narrow down your location.

### Pro Tips for Playing Investigative Roles
1. **Secure Crime Scenes Early:** When playing as the Forensic Specialist, lock down entry points immediately to prevent the Kira faction from wiping footprints.
2. **Watch the Line of Sight:** If you are playing the Master Detective, avoid standing out in clear view during major town meetings. Note Wielders need direct visual confirmation to target you.
3. **Form Two-Man Squads:** Never roam alone. Pairing an intel role with an armored role like the Deputy Guard significantly reduces sudden ambushes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best BloxNote characters for beginners?
For new players, the **Deputy Guard** on the investigative side and the **Shadow Disciple** on the Kira side are ideal. The Deputy Guard provides high durability and straightforward protection tasks, while the Shadow Disciple allows you to assist your team through vision scouting without the pressure of managing the Death Note directly.

### How do you unlock new BloxNote characters?
Characters and archetype variations are unlocked using credits earned from completing round objectives, winning matches, and leveling up your overall account rank. Certain specialized cosmetics and role badges are tied to specific in-game achievement milestones.

### Can BloxNote characters be customized with custom abilities?
While base abilities are locked to maintain competitive balance between factions, players can equip passive perk cards, custom investigative kits, and cosmetic overhauls as they increase their role mastery level.

### How does the Note Wielder identify other BloxNote characters?
The Note Wielder must obtain a player's full in-game handle through direct line of sight, gathering dropped ID badges, or having their Shadow Disciple relay target names using the Crimson Vision ability.
