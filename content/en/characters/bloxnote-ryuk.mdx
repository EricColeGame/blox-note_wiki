## Best Strategies for Playing With and Against Ryuk

Whether you are commanding **BloxNote Ryuk** or trying to deduce which opponent is harboring him, following a strict tactical checklist is crucial.

### Top Strategies for the Notebook User
- **Secure Apple Spawns Early:** Collect map apples before engaging in major write-offs. The cooldown reduction allows you to confirm multi-target eliminations before alarms trigger.
- **Bait False Positives:** Keep Ryuk positioned near neutral choke points. Since only specific players can see him, observing who reacts to Ryuk's model helps you identify opponents holding scraps of notebook paper.
- **Time the Realm Shift:** Save your Realm Shift ultimate for high-priority targets like the Lead Detective to prevent them from sharing crucial evidence with the lobby.

### Counter-Strategies for Investigators
- **Watch Health Bars:** If an opposing player's maximum health suddenly drops to 50% without combat, they have most likely purchased the Eye Deal from **BloxNote Ryuk**.
- **Deny Apple Resources:** Pick up or destroy apple spawns in high-traffic zones to prevent the notebook holder from stacking speed and cooldown buffs.
- **Exploit the Intangibility:** Remember that Ryuk cannot physically body-block attacks aimed at his contractor. Focus your fire directly on the player avatar.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do you get Ryuk in BloxNote?
You can access **BloxNote Ryuk** by obtaining the Death Note item during a match, unlocking his avatar skin through seasonal character crates, or equipping his specific Shinigami mastery tree in the game lobby.

### Can other players kill or banish Ryuk?
No. Ryuk is an immortal supernatural entity and cannot take damage from weapons or spells. However, eliminating the human player contracted to Ryuk will cause the Shinigami to despawn from that active area.

### Is the Shinigami Eye Deal worth taking in every match?
Not always. In competitive lobbies featuring skilled snipers or burst-damage detectives, reducing your maximum health to 50% can lead to rapid elimination. It is best used in fast-paced modes where speed matters more than defense.

### What happens if I fail to feed Ryuk apples in BloxNote?
If Ryuk's apple meter completely empties, you lose all passive movement and cooldown buffs. Community reports suggest that prolonged neglect may also cause Ryuk to provide visual distractions or auditory cues that give away your stealth position.
