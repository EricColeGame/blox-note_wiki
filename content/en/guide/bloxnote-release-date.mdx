## Core Gameplay Mechanics: Kira vs. L Roleplay

Unlike standard battle-arena anime games on Roblox that rely heavily on button-mashing skill trees, BloxNote focuses on social deduction, tactical stealth, deduction trees, and atmospheric roleplay. The core gameplay loop revolves around the battle of wits between Kira (wielding the notebook) and the investigative task force headed by L.

### Key Roles and Gameplay Overview

Understanding how roles function before the **BloxNote release date** gives you a major strategic edge during your initial matches.

| Role | Primary Objective | Key Abilities / Tools | Win Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kira** | Eliminate targets while avoiding identification | Death Note interface, name gathering, custom cause of death | Eliminate all detectives or reach target body count |
| **L (Lead Detective)** | Uncover Kira's identity through evidence | Investigation board, surveillance, suspect tagging | Correctly identify and detain Kira |
| **Task Force Investigator** | Gather clues and protect high-value targets | Questioning tools, escort protocols, alibi verification | Assist L in narrowing down suspect lists |
| **Civilian / Student** | Survive rounds and report suspicious behavior | Basic interaction, task completion, witness testimony | Help investigators or survive the trial period |

### The "L Transfer" System Explained

One of the standout mechanics highlighted during developer previews (specifically in Development Part 37) is the proprietary **L Transfer System**. In classic social deduction games, eliminating the team leader frequently creates an instant win for the villain. 

In BloxNote, if the active L player is eliminated or fails an investigation phase, their role and gathered deductive intel can transfer down to an eligible Task Force successor (such as Near or Mello archetypes). This system keeps matches dynamic, competitive, and unpredictable until the final seconds.
