## The Vision Behind BloxNote: Architecture and Core Design

The development team set out to recreate the tense atmosphere of supernatural investigative anime within the flexible framework of the [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com). Rather than building a generic battle arena, the creators prioritized psychological warfare, stealth, evidence collection, and deduction.

Player experience and community reports highlight that the game revolves around asymmetric multiplayer loops. One side must uncover identities through behavior analysis and clue tracking, while the other systematically eliminates targets without getting caught on surveillance or leaving forensic footprints.

### Core Design Pillars

| Design Pillar | Implementation in Game | Intended Player Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Psychological Tension** | Hidden roles and anonymous writing mechanics | Constant paranoia and strategic bluffing |
| **Information Asymmetry** | Scrambled logs, private notes, and limited vision | Rewarding deductive logic over pure combat |
| **Dynamic Round Timers** | Variable investigation phases based on deaths | Escalating urgency as the clock runs down |
| **Evidence Forensics** | Fingerprint scanning, CCTV footage, and eyewitnesses | Encouraging methodical detective work |

## Role Breakdown and Gameplay Archetypes

To succeed in BloxNote, players must master both sides of the investigative divide. The development team has fine-tuned the balance between wielders of the supernatural notebook and the detective task force.

### The Supernatural Faction (The Notebook Wielders)

The primary goal of the notebook wielder is to eliminate high-value targets while deflecting suspicion onto innocent bystanders.

- **Name Acquisition:** Wielders must obtain both the accurate display name and avatar face identification of their target.
- **Cause of Death Customization:** Advanced wielders can specify time delays and death causes to orchestrate elaborate alibis.
- **Shinigami Eyes Ability:** An optional high-risk trade-off where half of the player's round health or timer is sacrificed in exchange for immediate player identification above character avatars.

### The Detective Task Force (Investigators & Citizens)

The investigative faction relies on communication, forensic tools, and behavioral observation to pinpoint the rogue player before the lobby is wiped out.

- **Forensic Gathering:** Checking fallen bodies for proximity clues and timeline timestamps.
- **Security Monitoring:** Accessing localized CCTV feeds to establish alibis during reported incidents.
- **Vocal Accusation Phase:** Initiating emergency tribunal votes based on gathered physical evidence.

## System Mechanics: Notebook Rules and Counterplay

The **BloxNote dev** pipeline prioritizes strict mechanical interactions to ensure that no single role becomes unbeatable. Understanding the internal rule set is vital for surviving tough matches.

### Notebook Mechanics & Limitations

1. **Sight Requirement:** You cannot write the name of a player whose avatar face has not been directly in your field of view during the current phase.
2. **Spelling Accuracy:** Misspelling a target's username incurs a cooldown penalty and alerts nearby investigators with a faint heartbeat audio cue.
3. **Delay Scheduling:** Setting a delayed death allows you to mingle in crowds when the victim falls, granting a solid alibi.

| Mechanic Feature | Default Parameter | Tactical Usage | Counterplay Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Writing Time** | 3.5 Seconds | Requires staying stationary in secluded areas | Ambush quiet rooms and check isolated corners |
| **Heart Attack Timer** | 40 Seconds (Default) | Standard elimination method | Watch player movement changes before collapse |
| **Shinigami Vision** | Instant Name Tags | High-speed target acquisition | High-visibility targets should stay in large groups |
| **Notebook Drop on Death** | Immediate Physics Spawn | Pass the mantle to an Accomplice | Guard fallen suspects to secure evidence |

## Actionable Strategies for Mastering BloxNote

Whether playing as an investigator or the elusive wielder, standard gaming reflexes are secondary to game sense, timing, and psychological positioning.

### Top Strategies for Notebook Wielders

- **Never Stare Directly:** Looking straight at a target for an extended period is the fastest way to get flagged by observant detectives. Use third-person camera angles around walls to capture facial profiles.
- **Stage Staggered Eliminations:** Avoid eliminating players as soon as your cooldown resets. Irregular intervals break predictive patterns used by forensic analysts.
- **Leverage Group Confusion:** Execute your actions when large crowds gather near task terminals or during power outages.

### Top Strategies for Investigators

- **Establish Buddy Systems:** Move in pairs of trusted players. If one player dies, the last person seen with them is immediately placed under suspicion.
- **Cross-Reference Log Timestamps:** When a death is reported, immediately verify which players were outside camera range during that exact 40-second window.
- **Bait the Shinigami Eyes:** Have high-priority targets change cosmetics or wear accessories that partially obscure facial silhouettes, forcing the wielder to commit to riskier close-range interactions.
