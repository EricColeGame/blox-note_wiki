## The Core Round Loop in BloxNote

A typical match in BloxNote is fast-paced, typically lasting between four and six minutes. Each round is divided into distinct, predictable phases. Mastering this structure ensures you are never caught off guard when a body drops or when the emergency alarm rings.

### Phase Breakdown

1. **Role Cinematic & Spawning:** Every player loads into a central area. You are assigned your secret identity alongside a specific win condition.
2. **Free-Roam Exploration:** Investigators and Civilians scatter across the map to complete mini-game tasks, while Kira stalks targets to write their names down.
3. **Emergency Meetings & Trials:** Players call meetings by reporting a fallen player or using the emergency button. Discussion ensues, votes are tallied, and the accused player is ejected.
4. **Resolution & Reveal:** The round concludes once a win condition is fulfilled, revealing the identity of Kira and their accomplice.

| Round Phase | Average Duration | Primary Objectives | Critical Win Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Role Assignment** | 5–10 Seconds | Check role card and secondary goals | Mental preparation |
| **Free-Roam Phase** | 2–4 Minutes | Finish tasks or hunt isolated players | Task meter progress / Kill count |
| **Meeting Phase** | 30–60 Seconds | Share alibis, analyze scan data, vote | Ejection accuracy |
| **Post-Round Reveal**| 5–10 Seconds | Review player identities and stats | Learning opponent patterns |

## All Four Roles and Their Win Conditions

To succeed when exploring **BloxNote how to play**, you must understand the asymmetric balance between the four playable roles. Each character views the map through a completely different strategic lens.

### 1. Kira (The Killer)
Kira begins the round with the Death Note in their inventory. Your primary objective is to eliminate players by writing their names to hit a required kill threshold before the civilian side finishes their tasks. 

Writing a name triggers a brief animation followed by an elimination. However, you face a strict cooldown between kills. Community reports emphasize that burning your cooldown in the first 30 seconds often leaves you vulnerable to early Investigator tracking.

### 2. Kira Follower (The Backup)
The Follower operates as Kira's secret ally. While you do not start with killing capabilities, you inherit the Death Note if Kira gets voted out or caught, allowing your team to stay alive in the match. Your early-game job is to sow confusion and create fake alibis for Kira.

### 3. Investigator (The Detective)
Investigators look identical to standard Civilians but carry a concealed proximity scanner. When you walk close to Kira, your scanner alerts you. You must carefully maneuver into position, detect Kira, and bring verified evidence to the meeting table without revealing your role prematurely.

### 4. Civilian (The Task Force)
Civilians are the backbone of the lobby. While you lack special tools or offensive abilities, you control the shared task meter. By finishing interactable puzzles around the facility, you place immense time pressure on the killer team.

| Role | Starting Gear | Win Condition | Recommended Playstyle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kira** | Death Note | Eliminate targets to hit kill threshold | Stealthy, isolated hunting |
| **Kira Follower** | None | Assist Kira; inherit Death Note upon Kira's exit | Deceptive support, alibi creation |
| **Investigator** | Proximity Scanner | Identify and vote out all Kira users | Methodical scouting, controlled calls |
| **Civilian** | None | Completely fill the shared task meter | Rapid task completion, buddy system |

## Task Meter Mechanics and Civilian Strategy

The shared task meter serves as the invisible round clock. Every completed wiring puzzle, item delivery, or arcade terminal directly fills this bar. If the task meter reaches 100%, the civilian side secures an instant victory, regardless of how many lives were lost.

### When to Call Meetings vs. When to Do Tasks

A common mistake when learning **BloxNote how to play** as a Civilian is calling unnecessary emergency meetings. Every second spent chatting at the table is time stolen from the task meter.

* **Ahead on Tasks (Meter > 70%):** Do not hit the button unless a body is right in front of you. Focus purely on finishing the final objectives.
* **Behind on Tasks (Meter < 50%):** Rely on Investigators to provide solid scanner intel to eliminate the killer before they wipe the lobby.
* **Stalled Match (3+ Kills):** Group up tightly near known Investigators to protect each other while completing final assignments.

| Game State | Task Meter Status | Recommended Team Response | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dominant Lead** | 80% – 99% | Spread out safely, ignore emergency calls, finish tasks | Very Low |
| **Balanced Pace** | 50% – 79% | Work in pairs, report bodies immediately | Moderate |
| **Critical Deficit** | 25% – 49% | Force meetings, rely heavily on Investigator scans | High |
| **Crisis Mode** | Under 25% with kills | Stack as a full group, bait scanner pings | Maximum |

## 5 Essential Tips for Beginners

If you want to immediately boost your win rate, implement these practical tips grounded in general player experience:

1. **Establish an Early Alibi:** Spend the first 45 seconds near recognizable players working on visible tasks so multiple people can verify your whereabouts.
2. **Never Vote on Solo Accusations:** Unless an Investigator confirms a proximity ping with timestamp evidence, skipping the vote is almost always safer than ejecting an innocent teammate.
3. **Watch the Meter, Not the Kill Count:** Even if Kira secures two fast eliminations, a task meter sitting above 80% means you are winning. Do not panic; finish your mini-games.
4. **Delay Body Reports Tactically:** Rushing to hit the report button instantly pins you to the crime scene. Step back slightly to verify your surroundings before sounding the alarm.
5. **Master Kira's Elimination Range:** When playing Kira, never pull out the Death Note in high-traffic corridors. Isolate targets in dead-end rooms to avoid getting caught mid-animation.

| Mistake to Avoid | Why It Fails | Better Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chain-killing early** | Triggers rapid emergency meetings | Space out kills across different map zones |
| **Calling meetings without info** | Pauses task meter fill rate | Only call with hard evidence or body reports |
| **Wandering entirely alone** | Makes you an easy target for Kira | Use the buddy system with trusted players |
| **Ignoring the scanner** | Wastes the Investigator's main advantage | Check proximity alerts in tight choke points |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important rule in BloxNote how to play effectively?
The single most important rule is managing the task meter. If you are on the Civilian side, completing tasks is your primary win condition. If you are Kira, disrupting task runners and securing stealthy eliminations before the bar fills is essential.

### How does the Investigator know who Kira is?
Investigators carry a hidden proximity scanner. When an Investigator gets close to Kira during the free-roam phase, the device lights up. The Investigator can then use this scan data to lead the discussion during the next meeting.

### Can Kira win if all Investigators are eliminated?
Yes. Kira wins by reaching the required kill threshold, successfully taking down the final Investigator, or preventing the task meter from reaching 100% before the round timer runs out.

### How do I practice BloxNote how to play without getting eliminated early?
Start your first few matches by playing conservatively as a Civilian. Stick close to other players, focus on learning where task terminals are located, and observe how experienced players question suspects during emergency meetings.
