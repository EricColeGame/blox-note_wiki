## The Notebook Mechanics: Rules, Conditions, and Timing

The centerpiece mechanic in the **BloxNote demo** is how the notebook functions during live matches. Community reports emphasize that writing names is not an instant one-click win button; it requires careful observation, knowledge of player names, and tactical positioning.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Using the Notebook

1. **Acquire Visual Identification:** You must look directly at a player character to register their visual avatar face into your personal memory log.
2. **Obtain the True Username:** Display names can be deceiving. The notebook requires the exact account handle or in-game assigned alias.
3. **Select Cause of Death:** Default eliminations trigger after a 40-second delay via cardiac arrest. Custom causes allow unique delays and environmental distractions.
4. **Manage Writing Animations:** Pulling out the notebook triggers a subtle posture shift, which sharp-eyed detectives can spot from across the room.

| Execution Type | Delay Time | Noise Level | Special Condition Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cardiac Failure (Default)** | 40 Seconds | Silent | Requires visual face identification and full username. |
| **Accidental Hazard** | 60 Seconds | Moderate | Target must be within 15 studs of an environmental hazard. |
| **Controlled Movement** | 30–90 Seconds | Very Low | Target walks to a designated coordinate before falling. |
| **Mass Target Queue** | Staggered (20s each) | High | Consumes substantial spiritual energy meter. |

## Essential Strategies for Playing the BloxNote Demo

Whether you are carrying the notebook or hunting down its owner, subtle mistakes will cost you the game. Below are proven playstyles shared during community playtesting sessions.

### Top Strategies for the Notebook Wielder

- **Blend in with Task Runners:** Never wander aimlessly. Always simulate doing mundane civilian mini-tasks to maintain a solid alibi.
- **Space Out Eliminations:** Rapid eliminations allow the Lead Detective to cross-reference player locations at the time of each death.
- **Use Third-Party Sightlines:** Hide behind corners or through tinted office windows to register faces without exposing your own model.

### Top Strategies for the Detective Team

- **Cross-Reference Timelines:** Note where every player was 40 seconds prior to a reported casualty.
- **Set Up Bait Encounters:** Have an undercover officer fake AFK behavior in a semi-isolated zone while monitored by hidden cameras.
- **Watch Writing Postures:** Look out for players standing still in isolated corridors with idle hand positions.

| Tactical Scenario | Recommended Wielder Action | Recommended Detective Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clustered Lobby (5+ Players)** | Avoid writing; line-of-sight risk is extremely high. | Gather the group in well-lit common areas to track animations. |
| **Power Outage Event** | Trigger an elimination during panic to obscure the timer. | Sweep backup generator rooms immediately with flashlights. |
| **Solo Investigation Lead** | Eliminate the isolated deputy before they transmit alibis. | Pair up in two-person buddy systems before entering dead zones. |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What platforms support the BloxNote demo?
The demo is currently optimized for PC and mobile devices running the latest client versions. Full controller support for console players is anticipated in subsequent releases based on developer announcements.

### How do I get access to the BloxNote demo?
You can access the preview experience by searching for the official game title directly on the platform or joining verified community testing groups where server links and build announcements are regularly shared.

### Can the Lead Detective make a direct arrest without evidence?
No. In the **BloxNote demo**, false arrests penalize the detective team with reduced reputation, temporary movement stuns, or complete removal of arresting authority to prevent random griefing.

### Does the Notebook Wielder lose if their book is stolen?
In current demo builds, the notebook is locked to the wielder's character inventory. However, dropped evidence pages or suspicious handwriting logs can be found by investigators if the Wielder is careless with their write-ins.
