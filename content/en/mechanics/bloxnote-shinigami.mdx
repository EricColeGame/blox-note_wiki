## Core BloxNote Shinigami Mechanics and Lore

In supernatural social deduction and survival environments, Shinigami operate under strict metaphysical laws. Originating from a barren, desolate realm filled with bone structures and cosmic observation points, these entities monitor the mortal world through specialized portals.

Unlike physical players, a Shinigami possesses several inherent traits:
* **Intangibility and Phasing:** They can pass through physical obstacles, walls, and structures effortlessly.
* **Selective Visibility:** Only players who have physically touched a notebook bound to that specific death god can perceive or hear them.
* **Physical Immunity:** Conventional physical attacks, weapons, or traps inflict zero damage on a death god; any wounds regenerate immediately.
* **Flight and Mobility:** Retractable wings enable unhindered vertical and aerial movement.

## Shinigami Eye Deal Mechanics: Costs, Benefits, and Strategy

The defining transaction between a player and their death god companion is the infamous Eye Deal. By choosing to commune with your **BloxNote shinigami**, you trade half of your remaining lifespan in exchange for absolute visual dominance over other players.

### How the Eye Deal Operates

When the deal is sealed, the death god places their hand over the user's head, instantly unlocking the visual overlay. This gives the user the supernatural ability to see real player identities, account tags, and active lifespan timers directly above their avatars.

```
[Standard Vision: Obscured / Disguised Names]
                     │
            (Shinigami Eye Deal)
                     │
                     ▼
[Shinigami Eyes Active: True Name + Remaining Lifespan HUD]
```

### Eye Deal Cost-Benefit Analysis

| Feature | Base User (No Deal) | Shinigami Eye Deal Active | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Identification** | Requires alias deduction or physical clues | Instant true name reveal above player avatar | Eliminates guesswork against disguised foes |
| **Lifespan Tracking** | Hidden | Fully visible lifespan numbers | Identifies priority targets and fellow notebook holders |
| **Survival Penalty** | 100% baseline survivability | -50% maximum round/lifespan timer | Drastically reduces your margin for error |
| **Stealth Detection** | Standard line-of-sight | Bypasses basic cosmetic disguises | Hard-counters identity-swapping tactics |

### Strategic Tips for Making the Deal

1. **Wait for Mid-Game Stalemates:** Making the trade immediately at the start of a match cuts your survival buffer unnecessarily. Community reports suggest waiting until investigators begin cornering your suspected identity before trading for instant elimination power.
2. **Counter Disguise Specialists:** If enemy investigators use identity-masking abilities or false aliases, activating the eyes completely nullifies their stealth.
3. **Calculate the Lifespan Threshold:** Always verify that the remaining round time allows you to secure required eliminations before the halved timer expires.

## Advanced Tactics and Player Experience Insights

According to community reports and veteran player experience, mastering your **BloxNote shinigami** interactions separates casual notebook users from unbeatable masterminds. 

### Avoiding Visual "Ghost Tracking"
Even though investigators cannot see your death god, they *can* observe your pathfinding. If you consistently turn toward empty corners where your entity hovers or walk awkwardly to avoid phantom hitboxes, perceptive players will notice. Always navigate corridors and open spaces with completely natural movement.

### Notebook Passing Tactics
Passing a notebook to an ally grants them visibility of the bound entity while stripping your own special perks unless you retain a scrap of paper. You can use this mechanism to create false leads:
* Allow an investigator to touch a depleted notebook page to confuse their read on which entity is tied to which active threat.
* Use multiple notebooks to tether two different death gods across split squad members, dividing detective focus.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if I reject the Eye Deal from my BloxNote shinigami?
If you reject the deal, you retain 100% of your current lifespan and round timer. However, you will have to deduce player names through chat logs, visual badges, or environmental clues rather than seeing their true names automatically displayed above their heads.

### Can other players see my BloxNote shinigami during a match?
No. Other players cannot see or hear your death god companion unless they have physically touched the specific notebook bound to that entity. If an investigator touches your book, they gain instant visual detection of your companion.

### Can a Shinigami directly attack or eliminate investigators for me?
Under supernatural law, a death god cannot directly attack, physically harm, or eliminate mortals on your behalf. All eliminations must be executed through valid notebook entries made by the human user.

### Why do some death gods die when saving humans?
As demonstrated by Gelus and Rem in source lore, a death god's fundamental purpose is to take human life to extend their own. If an entity uses their power specifically to prolong a mortal's life out of love or protective instinct, they violate core cosmic laws and are instantly reduced to ash.
