## Core Mechanics of the BloxNote Death Note

The primary gameplay loop revolves around identity, names, and precise execution methods. Unlike standard asymmetric survival games where killers chase survivors with physical weapons, BloxNote emphasizes information control. 

To eliminate a target, the notebook user must satisfy specific conditions:
- **Full Display/Real In-Game Name**: Typing errors will cause the command to fail.
- **Visual Contact or Line-of-Sight Window**: Depending on the server mode, knowing the target's face or avatar silhouette is required.
- **Cause of Death Specifications**: If left blank, default heart failure triggers automatically after a predetermined timer (usually 40 seconds).

## Strategic Playbook: Investigator vs. Notebook User

Mastering BloxNote requires understanding both sides of the mental chess match. Below are proven tactical playbooks tailored to each faction.

2. **Manipulate Death Conditions**: Use situational causes of death (such as room traps or fake suicides) to draw investigators away from safe zones.
3. **Carry Decoy Notebooks**: Keep secondary inventory slots occupied so inspect tools do not immediately flag empty utility hands.
4. **Target the Informants First**: Eliminate civilian witnesses who actively communicate in server chat before attacking high-profile detectives.

### Tactics for the Detective Task Force
1. **Monitor Name reveal Windows**: Track who wanders near target players right before the 40-second countdown initiates.
2. **Implement Disguise Rotations**: Switch accessories or outfit presets to prevent the **BloxNote death note** holder from acquiring clean visual locks.
3. **Control Central Surveillance**: Guard CCTV and surveillance terminals to catch notebook writing animations in real time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you get the BloxNote death note in a match?
The notebook is assigned randomly at the start of a round to the player chosen as the primary antagonist. In specific game modes or custom servers, an extra notebook can spawn as a rare world drop or through Shinigami pact events.

### What happens if you misspell a name in the BloxNote death note?
If you enter an incorrect username, the execution command fails, and your write cooldown initiates without eliminating the target. In certain competitive lobby configurations, repeated misspellings impose a temporary lock on the book.

### Can investigators survive a BloxNote death note entry?
Under standard rules, once a name is entered with proper conditions and the countdown begins, the elimination cannot be stopped. However, specific defensive roles equipped with specialized medical kits or divine charms can grant a one-time survival shield if applied before the timer reaches zero.

### Does using the BloxNote death note make noise?
Writing in the book produces a subtle quill scratching sound and a brief animation. Players standing within immediate proximity can hear this sound, making isolated areas the safest spots to log names.
