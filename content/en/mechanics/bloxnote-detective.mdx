## Spotting Kira: Behavioral Clues and Suspicious Tells

Catching Kira requires keen psychological evaluation. Because Kira players must mimic standard task completion while searching for the lead **BloxNote detective**, their movement patterns often betray their hidden role.

### Legitimate Investigator vs. Kira Behavior

| Behavioral Metric | Genuine Investigator | Suspected Kira |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task Engagement** | Remains stationary for standard puzzle durations | Fakes tasks by standing near consoles for irregular times |
| **Movement Patterns** | Moves directly between task nodes | Wanders corridors, lingering near solitary players |
| **Reaction to Deaths** | Regroups with known players to share info | Immediately diverts conversation or shifts blame |
| **Proximity Awareness** | Keeps safe distance from unknown players | Attempts to isolate individual players in dead ends |

### Actionable Interrogation Questions

When questioning suspicious players during emergency meetings or discussions, use structured inquiries:
- *"Which specific terminal did you just finish in the East Wing?"*
- *"Who did you pass on your way from the Security Room?"*
- *"Why did you leave the Node console before the puzzle completion prompt triggered?"*

Inconsistencies in their timeline are the quickest way for a **BloxNote detective** to confirm a suspect's guilt.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the primary role of a BloxNote detective?
The primary goal is to gather clues by solving puzzle minigames, analyze player movement patterns, identify the hidden Kira, and neutralize them before the detective team is eliminated.

### How does playing as L differ from playing as a standard investigator?
While both share the same faction goal, L possesses specialized access to deeper deduction mechanics but serves as Kira's primary elimination target. Investigators act as the protective vanguard and information gatherers for L.

### What should I do first when a round starts in BloxNote?
Immediately head toward low-risk minigame consoles to begin generating clues while observing which players hesitate or trail behind others without completing objectives.

### Can Kira fake completing puzzle minigames?
Yes. Kira players frequently stand near consoles to blend in. However, observant detectives can time their interactions or check clue progression bars to verify if the task was genuinely completed.
