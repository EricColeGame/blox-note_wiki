### Early Game: Securing Information

During the first 60 to 90 seconds, Kira is typically cautious, memorizing names and looking for isolated victims.

- **Establish Camera Dominance**: Quickly check the CCTV network to see who travels in pairs and who immediately slips into secluded corners.
- **Protect Your True Identity**: Avoid announcing yourself as the lead investigator in public chat unless server mechanics force role transparency.
- **Assign Trusted Escorts**: Pair yourself with a verified officer to deter direct ambush attempts.

### Mid Game: Analyzing the Kill Timeline

When the first elimination occurs, community reports indicate that the next two minutes determine the outcome of the match.

| Elimination Type | Clues Left Behind | Immediate Detective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Proximity Elimination** | Heavy residue, rapid departure trail | Sweep immediate corridors; check closest doors |
| **Remote Elimination** | Name-written victim, delayed effect | Check who had direct line-of-sight earlier |
| **Staged Accident** | Disrupted environment, misleading debris | Inspect security logs for pre-tampering |

### Late Game: The Final Confrontation

Once your suspect pool is narrowed down to two or three players, avoid isolating yourself. Force the remaining players into monitored zones where using an ability or notebook leaves unmistakable visual cues.

## Advanced Tactics: Outplaying Kira's Deception

Experienced Kira players rely on alibis, body blocking, and false accusations against innocent players. Elevate your gameplay in the **BloxNote detective mode** using these tactical maneuvers.

### Deception Detection Matrix

Use this matrix during interrogations to evaluate whether a player's behavior matches innocent civilian routines or Kira patterns.

| Observed Behavior | Potential Civilian Reason | Potential Kira Reason | Detective Countermeasure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lingering near spawn** | AFK / Checking menu | Waiting for names to appear | Test responsiveness with direct chat queries |
| **Following from afar** | Seeking safety in groups | Stalking for name acquisition | Turn corners abruptly; check if they mimic pathing |
| **Avoiding security cameras**| Unfamiliar with map layout | Avoiding log timestamps | Place discrete bugs along alternate routes |
| **Rapid room hopping** | Completing quick tasks | Hunting isolated targets | Coordinate with officers to set up a pincer intercept |

### Tactical Tips for High-Level Play

- **The Bait Strategy**: Intentionally reveal a "fake" high-priority target to see which suspect repositions toward them.
- **Silent Note Taking**: Never reveal your primary suspect in public chat until you have secondary confirmation. Tipping off Kira causes them to alter their kill schedule.
- **Audio Cue Tracking**: Footstep patterns change subtly when players sprint versus walk. Listen carefully around blind corners.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do first when loading into BloxNote detective mode?
Your first action should always be assessing player distribution across the map and securing a secure surveillance point. Avoid wandering alone into unmonitored zones like basements or utility corridors where Kira can target you without witnesses.

### How does BloxNote detective mode handle false arrests?
Making an incorrect arrest carries severe penalties, including temporarily disabling your investigative tools, losing authority points, or leaving your team without protection. Always verify clues through multiple sources before committing to an arrest.

### Can Kira fake evidence in BloxNote?
Yes, skilled Kira players can stage scenes or tamper with timing mechanics to frame bystanders. Cross-referencing camera timestamps against physical player speed is the most reliable way to disprove fabricated evidence.

### Is communication required to win as a detective?
While you can solve cases solo, community reports consistently show that detectives who coordinate with police escorts and share timely information have a significantly higher win rate against deceptive Kira players.
