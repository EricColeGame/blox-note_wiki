## Core Mechanics of the BloxNote Game Mode

At its foundation, the **BloxNote game mode** revolves around hidden identity, information gathering, and tactical elimination. When a round begins, players are assigned secret roles split primarily between the Killer faction, the Investigation team, and Neutral wildcards.

The gameplay loop balances daytime public interactions with covert actions. The Killer must discover player real names or IDs to write them into their notebook, while investigators gather clues, analyze movement patterns, and trigger emergency meetings to expose the culprit.

### Standard Match Structure

| Phase | Duration | Key Objectives | Available Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preparation Phase** | 30–45s | Map exploration, establishing alibis, initial positioning | Roam freely, inspect crime scene spawn points |
| **Investigation Phase** | 90–120s | Collect forensic clues, witness interactions, observe suspicious behavior | Complete tasks, track cooldowns, share intel |
| **Tribunal / Vote Phase** | 60s | Debate findings, cross-examine suspects, cast elimination votes | Voice/text chat debate, vote to convict or skip |
| **Sudden Death (Overtime)** | Variable | Triggered if key figures remain alive near the timer limit | High-stakes final confrontation mechanics |

According to community reports from early release lobbies, matches move fast. A single careless slip—such as being caught in line of sight when an elimination occurs—can destroy an otherwise flawless stealth strategy.

## Complete Role Breakdown and Win Conditions

Understanding your assigned faction determines your entire match strategy. The standard **BloxNote game mode** divides the lobby into distinct archetypes, each with unique toolkits and victory parameters.

## Killer Strategies: Writing, Deception, and Stealth

Playing the antagonist in the **BloxNote game mode** is exhilarating, but high-tier lobbies will punish aggressive, reckless play. To thrive as the Killer, you must master the art of delayed timing and misdirection.

### Advanced Killer Tactics

1. **Stagger Your Timers**: Never trigger eliminations immediately upon obtaining a name. Set custom timers so the victim falls while you are standing in a crowded room alongside the Detective, creating an ironclad alibi.
2. **Target Information Roles First**: Prioritize Forensic Officers before taking on the Chief Detective. Removing forensic support makes it much harder for innocents to reconstruct timelines.
3. **Exploit the Disciple's Sight**: Coordinate with your partner to pin false blame on active civilians by mimicking their movement patterns.

### Strategy Comparison: Aggressive vs. Methodical Play

| Strategy Type | Best Used When | Pros | Cons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed Blitz** | Small lobby size (6–8 players) | Ends rounds before forensics uncover leads | Highly vulnerable to early visual witness reports |
| **Delayed Execution** | Large lobby size (12–16 players) | Creates unbreakable alibis; confuses body timers | Slower pace gives investigators time to complete tasks |
| **Frame-Up Setup** | Experienced investigator presence | Causes innocents to vote out their own key roles | Requires precise timing and positioning |

Player experience indicates that the most successful Killers rarely sprint across open hallways. Blending in with task-focused civilians is the most reliable way to avoid early suspicion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the main objective in the BloxNote game mode?
The primary goal depends on your assigned faction. If you are an Investigator or Civilian, your objective is to deduce the identity of the hidden Killer and vote them out during Tribunal phases. If you are the Killer, your goal is to eliminate key targets using your supernatural notebook without revealing your identity.

### How do Killers learn other players' names in BloxNote?
Killers can discover names by getting close to targets, finding dropped identification cards, having their Disciple scout targets, or purchasing sight abilities from Neutral Shinigami entities at the cost of specific in-game penalties.

### Can innocent players defend themselves during a match?
Yes. While innocents cannot directly attack without an authorized verdict, certain roles like the Chief Detective can deploy protective wards, perform identity checks, and lock down specific rooms to prevent stealth eliminations.

### Is the BloxNote game mode beginner-friendly?
Yes, the core mechanics are straightforward to pick up. Newcomers should start in Classic Deduction lobbies, focus on completing assigned tasks, stay near groups of verified players, and observe Tribunal discussions to learn deduction fundamentals quickly.
