## Playing as Kira & Follower: Stealth, Alibis, and Framing

Succeeding as Kira requires blending into the background. The most effective killer in BloxNote is not the one running elaborate double-bluffs, but the one who appears completely ordinary and helpful.

A successful killer **BloxNote strategy** focuses on low-risk eliminations and manufacturing ironclad alibis before bodies are discovered.

```
┌────────────────────────┐     ┌────────────────────────┐     ┌────────────────────────┐
│  1. Establish Alibi    │ ──> │  2. Execute Target     │ ──> │  3. Rejoin Lobby       │
│  Touch task, chat casually    Steal ID in transition          Group up before report │
└────────────────────────┘     └────────────────────────┘     └────────────────────────┘
```

### Tactics for Kira and the Follower

- **The "Boring Player" Camouflage**: Actively discuss tasks, ask standard questions during meetings, and vote along with the clear majority. The less memorable you are, the longer you survive.
- **Coordinate with the Follower**: If your server features a Follower, use them as an alibi witness. Having a second player confirm you were in a different wing provides almost unbreakable cover.
- **Isolate in Transitions**: Never take an ID while standing in crowded task hubs. Catch players navigating hallways between rooms where line-of-sight is blocked.

### Kira Deception Playbook

| Deception Play | Execution Method | Best Used When | Counter-Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Fake Alibi** | Start a multi-step task, slip away briefly, return | Lobby is split across opposite sides of the map | Players monitoring task bar progress |
| **The Follower Shield** | Follower aggressively accuses an innocent to draw votes | Task meter is above 70% and time is running out | If Follower is ejected, Kira comes under heavy scrutiny |
| **The Reaction Bait** | Be the first to express confusion when a body is called | Early game to establish an innocent persona | Overacting can look unnatural to experienced players |
| **The Late-Game Split** | Target the most vocal detective during lights-out or chaos | Final 4–5 players remaining | Leaving a surviving witness nearby |

## Pro Tips to Instantly Improve Your Win Rate

- **Perspective-Taking Deduction**: Before accusing someone, ask yourself: *"If that player were Kira, would they realistically make that move right in front of witnesses?"* This simple filter prevents false accusations against clumsy innocents.
- **Release Information Incrementally**: If you hold crucial clues, share them one at a time. Dumping everything makes you an instant target and gives the killer room to spin a counter-narrative.
- **Prioritize the Meter Over Arguments**: When the task bar is nearly full, stop arguing in meetings. Vote skip quickly and finish your objectives to secure an effortless win.
- **Spectate and Learn**: If you are eliminated early, watch the surviving players. Recognizing behavioral habits across consecutive rounds will help you outsmart the same opponents in future matches.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most effective BloxNote strategy for catching Kira?
The most reliable method is tracking movement patterns and task bar progress rather than guessing based on chat behavior. Watch for players lingering near others without completing tasks, leaving terminals early without the meter moving, or consistently voting to skip without contributing information.

### When should you skip a vote in BloxNote?
You should always vote to skip when there is no verifiable evidence or when the task meter is close to 100%. Forcing a vote based on a hunch risks eliminating an innocent teammate, which shifts the vote arithmetic directly into Kira's favor.

### How should L manage information during meetings?
L should reveal observations in small, testable increments. Share specific facts that other players can verify (such as room locations) rather than declaring your role outright. This builds consensus while preventing Kira from identifying and eliminating you.

### What should Kira do if the Follower is caught?
If your Follower is exposed, vote with the majority to eject them. Defending a caught Follower immediately links you to them, destroying your alibi and ruining your late-game survivability.
